Text Case Converter

Change text between uppercase, lowercase, title case and sentence case without retyping.

Choose a conversion.

Choose the case that matches your task

UPPERCASE makes every letter uppercase, while lowercase converts letters to lowercase. Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each detected word, which is useful for simple headings. Sentence case lowercases the text and capitalizes the first detected letter after the start of the text or common sentence-ending punctuation.

Important formatting limits

Automatic case conversion cannot understand every writing convention. Proper names, acronyms such as PDF or AI, brand capitalization and language-specific title rules may need manual correction after conversion. Review the result before publishing or submitting important text.

The conversion runs in your browser. This tool changes capitalization only; it does not rewrite grammar, translate text or send the text to an AI model.
