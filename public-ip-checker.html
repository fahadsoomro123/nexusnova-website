What is my public IP?

See the public internet address your browser is currently using. The lookup runs only after you press the button; NexusNova does not add location, ISP or identity guesses.

Ready. No public-IP request has been sent yet.

When you press the button, your browser contacts the public ipify endpoint. Like any web request, that service can see the network address making the request. NexusNova does not request IP geolocation or ISP data.

Public IP vs local IP

Your public IP is the address internet services generally see for your connection. Devices on a home or office network may also have private local addresses such as 192.168.x.x or 10.x.x.x; this checker does not attempt to enumerate those local addresses.

IPv4 and IPv6

The primary lookup uses an IPv4/IPv6-capable endpoint, so the result may be either format depending on your connection. A separate IPv4 lookup is attempted when available and is clearly labelled.

Privacy note

The tool is intentionally click-to-run. It does not start a public-IP lookup in the background, and it does not combine the result with a location database.
