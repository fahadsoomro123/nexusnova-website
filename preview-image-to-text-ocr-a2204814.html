OCR FAQ

Is OCR always exact?

No. Small text, handwriting, curved pages, low contrast and unusual fonts can produce errors. Review important extracted text before using it.

Why is the first scan slower?

The OCR engine and English language model are downloaded on first use. Later runs may be faster because the browser can cache those resources.

A practical OCR workflow

Start with the clearest source image you have. Crop away unrelated background if possible, keep the page upright, and avoid glare across letters. After extraction, compare names, dates, prices, serial numbers and punctuation against the image before copying or downloading the text. OCR is best treated as a fast transcription draft rather than an unquestionable source.

Why image quality changes recognition

OCR works by identifying visual patterns that resemble characters. Motion blur can merge letter edges, perspective distortion can stretch lines, and low contrast can make foreground text difficult to separate from the background. A screenshot of digital text will usually be easier than a dim handheld photo of a curved page. Enlarging a tiny source does not recreate detail that was never captured.

What formatting may be lost

The output is plain editable text. Complex table structure, columns, exact spacing, fonts, checkboxes and page layout may not survive recognition. If the source contains a table or form, compare each row and field with the image instead of assuming the extracted line breaks preserve the original structure.

Language and handwriting limits

This page currently loads the English OCR model only. Mixed-language documents or non-English scripts can be incomplete or incorrect. Handwriting, stylized logos and decorative fonts are also harder than ordinary printed text. The tool does not claim handwriting-grade or universal multilingual accuracy.

Privacy and processing

The selected image is processed in the browser by the OCR library; NexusNova does not upload the image to a separate OCR backend. The Tesseract library and English model are downloaded from their external hosting resources when needed, so a network connection is required for the first load of those resources.
