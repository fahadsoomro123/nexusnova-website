How NexusNova LIVE handles freshness

“Live” is the name of the hub, not a promise that every dataset updates every second. Currency reference rates are daily data. The international gold reference is fetched by a scheduled publishing workflow and shows its upstream timestamp. Pakistan fuel values carry PSO's own effective date. Weather values are model forecasts from MET Norway. Earthquakes use a cached USGS M2.5+ past-day feed and show both USGS feed time and NexusNova snapshot time.

Why cached data files are used

NexusNova refreshes market, forecast and event datasets in publishing workflows instead of making every visitor call upstream APIs or source pages. That keeps public pages lightweight, reduces unnecessary third-party requests and makes source times easy to audit.

Important limitation

Reference market data can differ from executable prices, weather forecasts can differ from observations, and earthquake parameters can be revised after further analysis. Always use the named source and relevant official authorities for high-stakes decisions.
