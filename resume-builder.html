How to use the resume builder

Start with your name, professional title and contact details, then add a short summary that explains the kind of work you do and the strengths most relevant to the role. Add skills as short, specific phrases. In the experience area, put the newest or most relevant role first and describe responsibilities or results in plain language. Add your education or certifications last, then read the preview from top to bottom before exporting.

Privacy and local draft storage

The form is designed for browser-side drafting. The current draft is saved with localStorage on this device so a page refresh does not immediately remove your work. The Clear draft button removes the saved draft from this browser. Avoid entering passwords, identity-document numbers, bank information or other sensitive data that does not belong on a resume.

Saving the resume as PDF

The builder uses the browser's print feature rather than uploading your resume to a PDF-generation server. Select Print / Save as PDF, choose the browser's PDF destination when available, review margins and page count, then save the file. Different browsers and printers can format page breaks slightly differently, so inspect the final PDF before sending it to an employer.

Keep the resume focused

Use specific responsibilities and measurable results where possible. A short, readable resume is usually easier to scan than a page filled with generic phrases. Review names, dates and contact details carefully before exporting.

This builder does not claim to guarantee employment, interview selection or applicant-tracking-system results. It is a formatting and drafting utility.

Quick resume checklist

  • Use a professional email address and current phone number.
  • Keep dates and job titles consistent with your records.
  • Prefer concrete achievements over vague claims where possible.
  • Remove spelling mistakes and unnecessary personal details.
  • Open the exported PDF once before submitting it anywhere.
