A pending referral is not the same as a verified referral or guaranteed reward. Signup itself does not mint a referral reward; any future activation reward must satisfy the applicable server-side verification and eligibility rules.

NexusNova does not promise a mining rate, halving stage, countdown, cash value or reward when trusted source data or a secure reward path is not available.
