Review the draft against the product

A useful policy starts with an inventory of what the product collects, why it is needed, which providers receive it, where it is stored, how long it remains and how deletion works. Account records, payments, analytics, support tools and mobile SDKs may create separate data flows. Remove any sample statement that does not describe your real system.

Terms should describe actual service scope, user responsibilities, intellectual property, billing, cancellation, support and termination. If payment or account deletion works through an app store or provider, the document must describe that process correctly. Do not promise a cancellation or rights-request route unless it is implemented and tested.

The generator creates an editable starting point only. Confirm applicable law, recipients, retention, legal basis and rights routes with appropriate review. Use the SaaS and mobile app privacy checklist to compare the draft with product behavior. A generated document is not legal advice.

Frequently asked questions

Can I publish the generated documents as-is?

No. Replace placeholders, compare every statement with your real data flows and legal terms, and arrange jurisdiction-appropriate review before publication.

What information should I enter?

Use the actual data collected, providers, retention rules, features, operator identity and a monitored contact address. Do not invent a compliance practice.

Does the generator certify legal compliance?

No. It creates editable templates, not legal advice or a compliance certification. Obligations depend on the product, organization and markets served.
