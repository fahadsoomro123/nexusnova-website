What “worldwide” means here

The forecast search is worldwide: MET Norway Locationforecast provides forecasts for any location on Earth. The satellite image is also global. Radar is different because ground-radar networks, access rights and coverage are regional; NexusNova shows the official/open radar sources it can use without inventing missing coverage.

Why not use a free-looking global radar API?

Some popular radar providers restrict their free API to personal, educational or non-commercial use. NexusNova is an ad-supported commercial website, so those feeds are not silently embedded. Where a compliant source is unavailable, the page says so instead of showing an unlicensed layer.

Forecast versus observation

Locationforecast is a model forecast. The nearest forecast hour is useful as a current weather estimate, but it is not an instrument reading from a nearby station. Satellite and radar imagery are also informational and should not replace official warnings or emergency guidance.
