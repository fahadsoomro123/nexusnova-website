Why NexusNova uses MET Norway

MET Norway's Locationforecast service provides worldwide forecasts under open licences that permit commercial use with attribution. NexusNova identifies itself to the service and caches responses to avoid unnecessary traffic.

Why this page says “forecast” instead of “live conditions”

Locationforecast is a weather-model forecast service. The nearest available forecast hour can be useful for a current snapshot, but it is not the same thing as an instrument reading from a nearby weather station.

Worldwide coverage without fake search

NexusNova currently publishes a fixed, cached worldwide-city set instead of pretending to offer arbitrary global search. An arbitrary location search would require a geocoding and on-demand forecast architecture with compatible commercial terms and responsible caching. Until that is finalized, the featured-city selector is the truthful zero-cost worldwide option.
