Review the generated structure before using it. A well-structured prompt can improve clarity, but it cannot guarantee factual or correct AI output.

What makes a useful AI prompt?

A good prompt usually tells the model what role to take, the exact task, relevant context, boundaries and the format you expect back. Those details reduce ambiguity and make the response easier to evaluate.

Use AI output carefully

AI systems can still make mistakes even with an excellent prompt. Verify important facts, calculations and decisions independently, especially when the topic affects money, health, law or safety.
