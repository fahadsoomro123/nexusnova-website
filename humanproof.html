Security & privacy

Keep the complexity behind a simple user flow.

The live browser interaction uses on-device face landmark analysis. A hardened production system also needs replay resistance, nonce handling, action hashes, backend-signed receipts, abuse controls, monitoring and independent review.

Short camera interaction

The interface is designed around brief live verification moments rather than long recording sessions.

Replay-aware architecture

Randomized challenges, timing checks, nonce binding and device signals form part of the security direction.

Action-specific proof

The goal is not only “a human was present,” but “a human approved this exact action.”

No identity claim by default

HumanProof is positioned around presence and conscious approval, not automatic real-world identity claims.

Realistic limitation

No liveness check is 100% secure or automatically deepfake-proof. Camera quality, accessibility needs, presentation attacks, compromised devices and implementation mistakes can affect results. HumanProof should be one risk-control layer alongside other appropriate controls.

FAQ

HumanProof, explained simply.

Is HumanProof face recognition or KYC?

No. The product is focused on showing that a live human consciously approved a specific action. It does not by itself establish legal identity or KYC status.

Does the public flow equal production-grade liveness?

No. It is a live browser interaction and evaluation surface. Production security requires stronger backend and anti-replay controls.

Do normal NexusNova tools become paid?

No. Normal NexusNova browser tools remain free. The listed prices apply to production HumanProof verification services.

Is the flagship credential included?

Yes for completed production results where a credential is issued. Preview or sample credentials are not proof of verification.

Can a business integrate HumanProof without a separate app?

The commercial direction includes verification links, an embedded web widget/SDK and a REST API as those production integrations become available.

Does HumanProof move or hold money?

No. HumanProof is an approval and verification layer. A bank, wallet, marketplace or app remains responsible for the underlying transaction.
