Split PDF

Extract selected pages from a PDF and save them as a separate document.

Page syntax

Use commas and ranges, for example 1-3,5 to keep pages 1, 2, 3 and 5.

Useful for

Removing unneeded pages, sharing a chapter or isolating pages for a form submission.
