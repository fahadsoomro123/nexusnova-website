A4 at 300 DPI

A4 is 210 × 297 mm. At 300 DPI it is roughly 2480 × 3508 pixels in portrait orientation.

DPI is print density

DPI describes dots per inch in print workflows. Pixel dimensions depend on both physical size and the chosen density.

FAQ

What is A4 size in pixels at 300 DPI?

A4 at 300 DPI is approximately 2480 × 3508 pixels in portrait orientation, or 3508 × 2480 pixels in landscape.

What is A4 size in pixels at 150 DPI?

A4 at 150 DPI is approximately 1240 × 1754 pixels in portrait orientation.

Is 300 DPI always required?

No. It is a common high-quality print target, but posters viewed from farther away can often use a lower effective density, while specialist printing can require different settings.

Pixel dimensions depend on DPI

A physical paper size does not have one universal pixel size. The pixel result changes with the DPI/PPI value, so choose a value that matches the print or screen workflow.

Check the destination requirement

For printing, use the printer or publisher requirement. For online forms, use the exact dimensions requested by that service instead of assuming a print DPI.
