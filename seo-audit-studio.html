Frequently Asked Questions

What does SEO Audit Studio check?

It checks metadata, headings, links, structured data, crawl-related signals, usability and available mobile and desktop performance measurements. Unavailable checks are labelled limited or unavailable, not passed.

What is included in the free preview?

The preview includes an overall grade only when the required evidence is available, seven summary categories and up to three key findings. Missing measurements are not made up.

How does the full report unlock?

The backend must verify the matching order and successful payment with the provider before unlocking the report. A redirect or client-side button alone cannot unlock it.

Does an SEO audit guarantee higher rankings?

No. An audit identifies observable issues and opportunities, but search rankings depend on content, competition, indexing and other factors. It cannot guarantee rankings or AdSense approval.
