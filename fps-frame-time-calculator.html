See how FPS translates into milliseconds per frame—a useful way to understand rendering responsiveness.

Convert FPS and frame time

60 FPS ≈ 16.67 ms per frame.

Why frame time matters

FPS is a rate; frame time is the time budget for one frame. A stable 120 FPS is about 8.33 ms per frame, while 240 FPS is about 4.17 ms. Lower frame time generally means more frequent visual updates, but smoothness also depends on consistency.
