How the CS2 to Valorant conversion works

CS2 and Valorant use different in-game sensitivity scales. A commonly used yaw-based conversion keeps approximate mouse distance for a full 360-degree turn similar. For CS2 to Valorant, divide the CS2 sensitivity by about 3.1818. For Valorant to CS2, multiply by about 3.1818.

Why the converted value can still feel different

Matching 360-degree distance does not make two games feel identical. Field of view, movement, weapon handling, acceleration settings, monitor distance and personal habits all affect perceived aim. Treat the converted number as a starting point, not a promise that both games will feel exactly the same.

Keep mouse DPI consistent while testing

The converter changes the in-game sensitivity number, not your physical mouse DPI. Keep DPI fixed for the first test so you can evaluate the conversion itself. If you change DPI and in-game sensitivity at the same time, it becomes harder to tell what caused the difference.

Check your eDPI too

Your effective DPI is mouse DPI multiplied by in-game sensitivity. It is useful when comparing settings inside the same game. Use the NexusNova eDPI Calculator for that calculation.

Tip: change one variable at a time and test the converted value in normal play before making small personal adjustments.
