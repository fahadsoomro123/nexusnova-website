CS2 ↔ Valorant Sensitivity Converter.

Convert mouse sensitivity between Counter-Strike 2 and Valorant while keeping the same basic 360-degree turn distance. No account and no upload required.

Common conversion: CS2 → Valorant ≈ divide by 3.1818. Valorant → CS2 ≈ multiply by 3.1818. Small rounding differences are normal.

How the CS2 to Valorant conversion works

CS2 and Valorant use different internal sensitivity scales. A commonly used yaw-based conversion keeps approximate mouse distance for a full 360-degree turn consistent. For CS2 to Valorant, multiply the CS2 sensitivity by about 0.3142857, which is the same as dividing by about 3.1818.

Example

A CS2 sensitivity of 1.20 converts to roughly 0.377 in Valorant. If you move the other way, a Valorant sensitivity of 0.377 converts back to roughly 1.20 in CS2.

Why the result may still feel different

Matching 360-degree distance does not make two games feel identical. Field of view, movement, weapon handling, acceleration settings, monitor distance and personal habits can all affect perceived aim. Treat the converted number as a reliable starting point, then make small adjustments if needed.

Check your eDPI too

Your effective DPI is mouse DPI multiplied by in-game sensitivity. It is useful when comparing settings inside the same game. Use the NexusNova eDPI Calculator to calculate it instantly.

Tip: change only one variable at a time. Keep your mouse DPI fixed while testing a new in-game sensitivity so you can tell what actually changed.
