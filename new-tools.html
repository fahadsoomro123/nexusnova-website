How to use NexusNova tools safely

  • Check important outputs. Browser calculators and generators are convenience utilities; verify results when money, legal requirements, deadlines or professional documents are involved.
  • Know when a third party is involved. Most local tools run entirely in your browser. The YouTube thumbnail page loads preview images from YouTube's image host, and its page explains that behavior.
  • Do not confuse estimates with exact model data. The AI Token Calculator deliberately labels its result as approximate because tokenizers differ by model.
  • Respect content rights. Public access to a thumbnail URL does not automatically grant permission to reuse the image.
  • Save your own work. The Invoice Maker has no cloud archive. Print or save the invoice before leaving the page.
