WHAT THE CUSTOMER PAYS FOR

A service, not just a camera screen.

The commercial value is the complete verified-action workflow: action context, live-human response, production validation and a digital result tied to the exact action.

Live-human approval

The user completes a brief natural-response challenge before a sensitive action can proceed. HumanProof does not by itself establish legal identity or KYC status.

Exact-action binding

The production architecture is designed to bind approval to the specific operation, such as a deploy, recovery, refund, export or high-risk transaction.

Trusted production result

The browser interaction alone cannot issue a verified result. Production issuance follows payment, server validation and the required trusted checks.

Digital receipt / credential

After successful completion, the customer receives an action reference and a digital HumanProof result or credential that can be kept with the workflow record.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you buy.

Is HumanProof the same as identity verification or KYC?

No. HumanProof is positioned around live-human presence and conscious approval of a specific action. It does not by itself establish legal identity.

Do free NexusNova tools become paid?

No. Normal NexusNova browser tools remain free. These prices apply only to production HumanProof verification services.

What does one verification credit mean?

One credit represents one production HumanProof verification that reaches the billable state defined in the service terms. Failed technical fulfilment is handled under the refund policy.

Is the flagship credential included?

Yes for completed production results where a credential is issued. Preview/sample credentials are clearly marked and are not proof of verification.

Why is pricing low?

These are early-access launch prices for a new product. NexusNova may revise pricing for future purchases as the production service matures.

Can businesses integrate HumanProof?

The intended model includes verification links, a web widget/SDK and API integration. Production availability is stated as each integration becomes ready.

Rollout transparency: NexusNova does not present the public camera challenge as a completed production verification. Paid checkout and production issuance must be connected to trusted server-side confirmation. Until Safepay merchant checkout is activated, the checkout page clearly identifies that status and does not simulate a real payment.
