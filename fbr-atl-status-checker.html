Why NexusNova does not display a guessed status

The official FBR IRIS 2.0 verification page currently provides an Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax) verification flow and asks for the taxpayer identifier plus a CAPTCHA. That means a normal public web page cannot responsibly pretend it has a direct, unrestricted live FBR result when it does not have an authorized machine-readable integration.

Important: Never enter a CNIC into a third-party page that promises an “instant FBR result” by bypassing the official security checks. Use the official FBR result as the authoritative status.

What the official route can tell you

FBR publishes the Active Taxpayer List and provides official ways to verify taxpayer status. The official portal is the place to use when the status affects withholding, filing, business documentation or another legal/financial decision.

How to use this tool

  1. Enter your 13-digit CNIC and let NexusNova validate the format.
  2. Open the official FBR verification page and complete its verification steps.
  3. Use the FBR result rather than an old screenshot, cached result or unofficial claim.

Privacy and limitations

The CNIC field is used only for local formatting and validation. NexusNova does not claim to verify your tax status itself, does not issue an FBR certificate, and does not replace the FBR portal, ATL data or official records.
