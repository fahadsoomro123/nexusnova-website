DAILY TOOL // RANDOM

Random Name & Item Picker

Paste one item per line and choose a random result locally in your browser.

Add at least one item.

Useful for quick everyday choices

Enter one option on each non-empty line. When you press Pick one, the page uses the browser's cryptographic random-number generator to choose an entry. The list is processed in your browser and does not need an account.

How repeated entries work

Each line is treated as one entry. If the same name or item appears twice, it effectively has two positions in the list and therefore a greater chance of being selected. Remove duplicates first when you want every distinct option represented once.

This lightweight picker is suitable for classroom participation, team order, choosing a game or deciding between ordinary options. It is not designed as an auditable system for regulated giveaways, gambling, legal selections or other high-stakes decisions that require certified randomness and a verifiable draw process.
