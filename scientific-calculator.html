When to use it

Useful for homework checks, quick engineering estimates and everyday mathematical work. Verify important technical calculations independently.

Scientific calculation basics

Scientific calculators extend ordinary arithmetic with functions used in algebra, geometry, statistics and science. Square roots require non-negative real inputs here, while logarithms require positive values. Power calculations use X as the base and Y as the exponent.
