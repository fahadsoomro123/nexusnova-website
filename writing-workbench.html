What the measurements mean

Words, characters and reading time answer different questions. Word counts are useful for writing limits, character counts matter for short-form fields, and reading time is only an estimate. None of these measurements is a quality score.

Why word counts can vary

Editors can disagree about hyphenated words, apostrophes, emoji and scripts that do not use spaces. This workbench uses whitespace-separated tokens for its simple word count; when a destination system has a stricter rule, verify the final text there too.

Clean for meaning, not for a target number

Whitespace cleanup can remove accidental repeated spaces and trailing blank lines, but deleting meaningful content just to hit a word quota can make the draft worse. Edit for clarity first and use measurements as constraints around the writing.

Case transformations are mechanical

Title and sentence case are convenience transforms. They do not understand every proper noun, brand style or language-specific capitalization rule, so review the transformed text before publishing.

Privacy boundary

The calculations and transformations on this page operate on the text currently in your browser tab. Avoid pasting secrets or confidential material into unfamiliar services; local processing reduces the need for a network upload but does not protect a device that is already compromised.
