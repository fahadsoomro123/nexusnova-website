Frequently asked questions

Does a hook guarantee views?

No. It creates draft opening ideas. Relevance, execution and platform distribution affect results.

Should every hook use clickbait?

No. A good opening makes a promise the clip actually fulfils and avoids unsupported claims.

What makes a short script useful?

A clear audience, specific hook, proof or demonstration, concise payoff and one honest next step.
