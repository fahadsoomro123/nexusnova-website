Understand the result

Three things matter most when you interpret a salary-tax estimate.

01

Progressive calculation

Lower portions of taxable income are taxed under their applicable lower slabs. The highest marginal rate is not applied to the whole salary.

02

Effective rate

The effective rate in the result compares total estimated annual tax with annual taxable salary, which is different from the marginal slab rate.

03

Take-home context

The displayed monthly after-tax figure is a mathematical estimate from this income-tax table, not a full payroll statement.

Checks & limitations

Use the calculator as a planning tool, then reconcile the result with your actual payroll and the applicable rules.

A

Salary-only scope

This page is for salaried-person slab estimates. Mixed income, business income, property income, capital gains, credits and special regimes need broader treatment.

B

Current-year basis

The page is explicitly labeled with Tax Year 2027 and its stated Finance Act 2026 basis so an old estimate is not mistaken for a current one.

C

Payroll can differ

Bonuses, taxable benefits, salary changes, prior deductions and year-end adjustments can make employer withholding differ from this simplified estimate.
