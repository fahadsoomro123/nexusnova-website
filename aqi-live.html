How the NexusNova PM2.5 AQI estimate is calculated

The scheduled publisher reads hourly PM2.5 observations through the OpenAQ API. It requires at least 18 valid hourly values in the rolling window and rejects stale data. The rolling concentration is truncated to one decimal place and mapped to the current U.S. EPA PM2.5 AQI breakpoints. NexusNova labels the result as an estimate because it is our calculation from third-party observations, not an AQI issued by a government authority.

Source and licensing checks

OpenAQ aggregates measurements from multiple providers and exposes licence metadata per monitoring location. The NexusNova publisher requires an OpenAQ-listed licence that explicitly allows commercial use before a station can be published. OpenAQ and the original provider remain attributed on the page.

Why a city may disappear temporarily

A monitoring device can stop reporting, hourly coverage can be incomplete, source access can fail or licence metadata can change. NexusNova fails closed in those cases instead of carrying forward an old value or replacing it with a modelled number.

Health and decision limitation

This page is informational. Sensor quality, siting and local conditions vary, and a single monitoring location may not represent the exposure at your home, school or workplace. For health guidance or emergency decisions, use relevant public-health and environmental authorities.
