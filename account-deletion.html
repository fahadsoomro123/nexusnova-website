Account & Privacy

Delete your NexusNova account

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your NexusNova account and associated personal data.

Last updated: 25 August 2026

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the same email address used for your NexusNova account.
  2. Use the subject NexusNova Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address of the account you want deleted.
  4. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion is completed.

Request account deletion

Data deleted or anonymized

After ownership verification, NexusNova will delete or anonymize account-related data it controls where legally and technically permitted. This can include account/profile information, cloud-backed app state, mining or reward state, tasks, referral data, a linked Telegram identity mapping and preferences associated with the account.

Data that may be retained

Limited records may be retained when reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, abuse prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution or other lawful obligations. Data independently controlled by third-party providers is handled under those providers' policies.

Permanent effect

Account deletion is intended to be permanent. Once deletion is completed, account access and recoverable NexusNova account data may no longer be available.

Privacy information

For more information about how NexusNova handles data, read the Privacy Policy. For other questions, use the Contact page.
