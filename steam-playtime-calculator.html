Steam Playtime Calculator

Turn your total Steam hours into average playtime per day, week or month. This tool does not connect to Steam or fetch account data.

Calculate average playtime

Enter total hours and a time period.

How many hours have I spent on Steam?

If you already know your total Steam playtime, enter that number above and choose how long you have been accumulating those hours. The calculator converts the total into estimated hours per day, week and month, which can make a large lifetime total easier to understand.

Total Steam playtime versus average playtime

Total Steam playtime is simply the sum of hours you enter. Average playtime spreads those hours across the period you choose. For example, 730 hours accumulated over one year is about 2 hours per day on average. The result is an estimate because the calculator does not know which days you actually played.

Steam playtime by month

Choose Months as the period unit if you want to estimate how your total hours translate across a number of months. If you only know a yearly total, the calculator also shows an approximate monthly average automatically.

Privacy and limitations

No Steam ID, login or API key is required. Nothing is fetched from your account, so you must enter the total hours yourself. This page is not affiliated with Valve or Steam and should not be treated as an official account-history report.

FAQ

Can this calculate my total Steam hours automatically?

No. It does not connect to Steam. Enter the total hours shown in your own Steam library or profile, then use the calculator to estimate daily, weekly or monthly averages.

Does “time wasted on Steam” mean the same thing as playtime?

Some people use that phrase casually, but this tool uses the neutral term playtime. It only performs arithmetic on the hours you provide and does not judge how that time was spent.
