Steam Playtime Calculator

Turn a total playtime number into an average per day or week. This tool does not connect to Steam or fetch account data.

Calculate average playtime

Enter total hours and a time period.

What this calculator does

Steam shows total hours for games, but totals are easier to understand when converted into an average. Choose the period over which those hours were accumulated. The result is only as accurate as the period you enter.

Privacy

No Steam ID, login or API key is required. Nothing is fetched from your account.
