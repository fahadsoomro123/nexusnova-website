CAREER GUIDE

Build a resume that is easy to scan.

Editorial owner: NexusNova Editorial Team

Focus the page on evidence, relevance and readability instead of decorative clutter.

Published 22 August 2026 • Updated 24 August 2026 • NexusNova Editorial Team

Start with the job, not with a generic template

A resume is more useful when it answers a specific employer's question: why should this person be considered for this role? Read the job description first and identify the experience, tools, responsibilities and outcomes that genuinely match your background.

Use a clear professional heading

Your name should be the easiest item to find. Under it, use a short professional title that reflects the role you are targeting. Contact details should be current and limited to useful information.

Write a summary with evidence

A good summary is short and specific. Instead of writing “hard-working professional seeking opportunities,” describe your relevant experience, strongest capability and the type of work you can contribute.

Turn responsibilities into outcomes where possible

Experience sections become stronger when they show what changed because of your work. A responsibility says what you were assigned; an achievement shows the effect.

Use numbers carefully

Numbers can make achievements easier to understand, but only use figures you know are accurate. Real counts, time saved, class sizes, customers handled or projects completed are useful when they are true and relevant.

Keep skills focused

A long skills list can dilute the important items. Prioritize skills that match the target role and that you can demonstrate.

Education and certifications

List relevant qualifications clearly with institution, program and completion year when appropriate. If a certification expires, do not present it as current after expiry.

One page is a useful constraint, not an absolute law

For early-career applicants and many general roles, one focused page is often easier to scan. People with extensive relevant experience may genuinely need more space.

Check before you send

  • Verify phone number and email address.
  • Check dates and employer names for accuracy.
  • Remove spelling mistakes and inconsistent punctuation.
  • Open the PDF and confirm nothing is cut off.
  • Use a professional filename.
  • Tailor the resume for the role.
