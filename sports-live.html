Powered by SportScore. NexusNova does not scrape score websites and does not relabel finished or scheduled matches as live.

How Pakistan-first sorting works

For cricket, matches mentioning Pakistan, Pakistan Super League, PSL or major Pakistan city/team names are prioritised within the SportScore response. If no Pakistan-relevant match is present, NexusNova shows the worldwide feed without inventing a Pakistan fixture.

What “live” means

Only matches whose upstream status is live or in-progress receive a LIVE badge. Scheduled matches keep their scheduled label and finished matches remain finished. Source timestamps are displayed when available.

Why a score can briefly lag

This page does not run its own scoring operation. A wicket, goal, point or match-status change first has to reach the upstream sports feed and then the browser refresh cycle. That means a broadcaster or league app can sometimes update before this page. For a disputed result, abandoned match, corrected score or official fixture decision, use the competition organiser or broadcaster as the final authority.

How to read the feed responsibly

Use the status label together with the teams, score and source timestamp instead of assuming every listed fixture is currently live. A scheduled event can appear beside an in-progress match, and a recently finished fixture can remain visible for context. NexusNova keeps those states distinct rather than presenting every returned item as a live event.

Source and fair use

SportScore documents a CORS-enabled attribution tier for football, basketball, cricket and tennis. NexusNova keeps the required visible attribution and uses modest client-side refreshes rather than bulk harvesting.
