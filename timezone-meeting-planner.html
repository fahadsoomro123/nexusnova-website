Global Timezone Meeting Planner

Plan a global meeting by choosing a date and time on your device, then compare that exact moment across three world time zones.

The entered date/time is interpreted in your device's local timezone. Daylight-saving rules are applied by your browser for the selected destination zones.

Quickly compare Pakistan time with Dubai, London, New York, Toronto, Singapore and other common business zones.

Daylight saving aware

No account or calendar connection is required. The planner formats dates directly in your browser.

How to use this global meeting planner

Enter the meeting date and time as it appears in your own local timezone, choose up to three destination time zones, then select Compare times. The planner shows the matching date, weekday and local clock time for each selected region so a remote team can check the same moment before sending an invitation.

World meeting planning across time zones

World meeting planning is easiest when everyone compares the same instant instead of manually adding or subtracting UTC offsets. This matters especially when teams span regions such as Pakistan, the UAE, the UK, Europe, North America, Singapore or Australia because daylight-saving changes can shift some locations during the year.

FAQ

What is a global meeting planner?

A global meeting planner compares one proposed meeting time across several world time zones. It helps distributed teams see the corresponding local date and time for each location before scheduling a call.

Why can London or New York shift by an hour?

Some regions use daylight-saving time. Their UTC offset changes during the year, so a fixed Pakistan meeting time can appear one hour earlier or later depending on the date.
