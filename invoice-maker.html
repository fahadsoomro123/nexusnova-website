How to make an invoice

  1. Enter your business or sender name and the client name.
  2. Add each item or service with quantity and rate.
  3. Add tax or discount only if it applies.
  4. Review the preview carefully.
  5. Select Print / Save PDF and choose your browser's PDF destination if you want a PDF file.
Before you send it: Use the Simple Invoice Checklist to review the sender, customer, invoice number, date, line items, math, payment notes and final PDF.

What this invoice maker does not do

This is a lightweight document calculator and print template. It does not send invoices, process payments, create tax filings, verify legal requirements or store accounting records. Invoice requirements vary by country and business type, so add any legally required tax, registration or address information before using an invoice professionally.

Privacy

The invoice fields and calculations run in your browser. This page does not intentionally send invoice content to a NexusNova server.

FAQ

Can I save the invoice as PDF?

Yes, on browsers that provide a Save as PDF destination in the Print dialog.

Does NexusNova keep my invoices?

No. This page does not include cloud storage. Save the PDF or print a copy before closing or resetting the page.
