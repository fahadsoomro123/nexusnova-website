Why this is not labelled real-time

Pakistan fuel prices are published with an effective date and can remain unchanged until the next official revision. NexusNova checks the source on a schedule, but the underlying price itself is not a second-by-second market quote.

How NexusNova prevents stale archive mistakes

The PSO page contains several products and archived price blocks. The publishing process only accepts a block when its effective date, Premier Euro 5 price and Hi-Cetane Diesel Euro 5 price are validated together. If the page structure changes or the latest block is ambiguous, publishing fails instead of guessing.

Outlet-price limitation

PSO notes that applicable freight charges can affect final selling prices at retail outlets. Use these figures as a current published reference and confirm the actual pump price at the location where you buy fuel.
