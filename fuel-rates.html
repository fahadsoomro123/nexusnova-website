Why these fuel figures use different freshness labels

Pakistan prices are published with an effective date and can remain unchanged until the next official revision. U.S. EIA gasoline and diesel figures are weekly national averages. NexusNova checks each source on an appropriate schedule and does not turn either dataset into a fake real-time feed.

How NexusNova prevents stale or mismatched source data

The PSO publisher validates the effective date, Premier Euro 5 price and Hi-Cetane Diesel Euro 5 price together. The separate EIA publisher validates the national U.S. row, weekly dates, gasoline and diesel values, and requires both U.S. products to carry the same latest data date. A failed validation stops publishing instead of inventing a value.

Do not compare the Pakistan and U.S. numbers directly

Pakistan values are PKR per litre, while U.S. values are USD per gallon. They also represent different national pricing systems. Convert both currency and volume units before making a numerical comparison, and remember that neither figure promises the exact price at a specific filling station.

Outlet and station-price limitations

PSO notes that applicable freight charges can affect final selling prices at retail outlets. EIA's U.S. figures are national averages including taxes and can differ substantially by state, city and station. Confirm the actual pump price where you plan to buy fuel.
