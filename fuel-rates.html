Why these fuel figures use different freshness labels

Pakistan prices are published with an effective date and can remain unchanged until the next official revision. U.S. EIA gasoline and diesel figures are weekly national averages. European Commission Oil Bulletin figures are weekly national consumer-price references for the 27 EU countries. NexusNova checks each source on an appropriate schedule and does not turn periodic data into a fake real-time feed.

How NexusNova prevents stale or mismatched source data

The PSO publisher validates petrol and HSD together. The EIA publisher validates the U.S. national row and matching weekly dates. The EU publisher validates the Commission workbook headers, EUR/1000 litre units, source date, Euro-super 95 and automotive diesel columns, and all 27 EU country rows. A failed validation stops publishing instead of inventing a value.

How EU prices are normalized

The Commission workbook reports the selected petrol and diesel products in euros per 1000 litres. NexusNova divides each validated source value by 1000 and displays euros per litre. It does not convert those figures into another currency or claim they are a specific station's pump quote.

Do not compare markets without matching units

Pakistan values are PKR per litre, U.S. values are USD per gallon, and EU values are EUR per litre. Convert both currency and volume units before making a numerical comparison, and remember that national references or averages do not promise the exact price at a particular filling station.

Outlet and station-price limitations

PSO notes that applicable freight charges can affect final selling prices at retail outlets. EIA U.S. figures are national averages including taxes. European Commission figures are country-level weekly consumer-price references including taxes. Local station prices can therefore differ from every section on this page.
