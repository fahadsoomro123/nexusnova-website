What Pulse is — and is not

Currency is a daily reference. Pakistan gold here is derived from international XAU/USD and USD/PKR, not a local Sarafa quote. Fuel uses published PSO references. Weather uses model forecasts from MET Norway. Earthquake activity uses a cached USGS magnitude 2.5+ past-day feed. Use the linked source pages for details and high-stakes decisions.

How to read a Pulse snapshot

Pulse puts several reference signals on one screen, but the cards do not share one universal update clock. Read the source date or forecast time shown on each card before comparing values. A daily currency reference, a fuel price with an effective date, a weather forecast and a recent-earthquake feed can all be current within their own schedules while carrying different timestamps.

Use the detailed pages when context matters

The compact view is designed for a quick check, not for explaining why a market, forecast or event changed. Open the linked Currency, Gold, Fuel, Weather or Earthquake page when you need the source method, units, freshness notes or limitations. For financial, travel or safety-sensitive decisions, confirm the underlying official or primary source as well rather than treating a dashboard snapshot as a guarantee.
