What CIDR means

The number after the slash is the network prefix length. A /24 leaves 8 host bits, giving 256 total addresses. Traditional IPv4 subnets from /0 through /30 reserve the network and broadcast addresses; /31 and /32 are special cases where this calculator reports all addresses as usable endpoints.

Privacy

This is a mathematical subnet calculator, not an IP-location lookup. The entered address stays in your browser and no network request is needed.
