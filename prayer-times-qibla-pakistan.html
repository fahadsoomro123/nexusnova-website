Calculation method

NexusNova uses the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi calculation settings: 18° for Fajr and 18° for Isha. You can choose Hanafi or Shafi Asr. Calculation code is provided by the open-source Adhan JavaScript library, pinned to version 4.4.6 under the MIT License.

Important limitation

Prayer timetables can differ by mosque, authority, elevation, local convention, safety offsets and scholarly method. For congregational prayer or a religious obligation, follow your trusted local mosque or authority if its timetable differs from this calculator.
