Calculation method

NexusNova uses the University of Islamic Sciences, Karachi calculation settings: 18° for Fajr and 18° for Isha. You can choose Hanafi or Shafi Asr. Calculation code is provided by the open-source Adhan JavaScript library, pinned to version 4.4.6 under the MIT License.

How to use the result carefully

Choose the city that best matches your actual location, then select the Asr method you follow. For example, changing from Karachi to Lahore changes the astronomical inputs, while changing Hanafi to Shafi can change the calculated Asr time. The Qibla value is a geometric bearing from the selected coordinates to the Kaaba; it does not guarantee that a phone compass is calibrated correctly.

If you use device location, compare the displayed city/location context and the calculated times with a trusted local timetable before relying on them. A difference of a few minutes can come from method, elevation, atmospheric assumptions, rounding or safety offsets used by local authorities.

Prayer-times calculation limitations

Prayer timetables can differ by mosque, authority, elevation, local convention, safety offsets and scholarly method. For congregational prayer or a religious obligation, follow your trusted local mosque or authority if its timetable differs from this calculator.

What this tool does not claim

NexusNova does not label these calculations as an official religious ruling or mosque schedule. The calculator is designed to make the calculation method visible, show the selected Asr convention and provide a Qibla bearing that you can independently cross-check.

Last updated / reference date: Prayer times are calculated from the selected location and stated method rather than fetched as an official mosque timetable. If required location inputs are unavailable, the page should not invent a local prayer time.
