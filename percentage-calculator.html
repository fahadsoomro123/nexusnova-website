How percentage calculations work

To find a percentage of a number, multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. To find what percent A is of B, divide A by B and multiply by 100. Percentage change compares the difference with the starting value.

Common uses

Percentages are useful for discounts, marks, growth, price changes, commissions and everyday comparisons.
