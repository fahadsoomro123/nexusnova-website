Refund Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This policy applies to paid NexusNova HumanProof digital services purchased directly from NexusNova Tools. Free browser tools on NexusNova Tools do not require a purchase and are not covered by this refund policy.

When a refund may be available

You may contact us for review when a duplicate payment is captured, when payment is successfully captured but the paid HumanProof service cannot be made available because of a NexusNova technical failure, or when an eligible business credit pack remains completely unused and the request is made within 7 calendar days of purchase.

Completed digital services

A HumanProof result or credential that has already been successfully issued is a completed digital service and is generally not refundable. Used business verification credits are also generally not refundable. This does not limit any mandatory rights that apply under relevant consumer law.

Failed or abandoned verification

A failed or abandoned camera challenge is not presented as a verified result. Where a payment has been captured, NexusNova will review genuine technical fulfilment problems separately from cases where the customer stops the flow, denies required browser permissions, uses an unsupported environment, or does not complete the requested verification steps.

How to request a refund

Email nexusnovatools@gmail.com or use the Contact page. Include the order reference, payment date, plan, amount and a short description of the issue. Do not send full card details, passwords or one-time codes.

Processing

Approved refunds are submitted back through the available payment route as soon as reasonably possible. The time for funds to appear can vary by card issuer, bank and payment provider.

Enterprise arrangements

Enterprise purchases may be governed by a separate written order, invoice or contract. Where such terms exist, the agreed commercial terms apply in addition to any rights that cannot legally be waived.

Information that helps a refund review

Keep the order reference, purchase date, plan name, charged amount and a short description of what happened. Screenshots of an error can be useful when they do not expose passwords, full card numbers, one-time codes or other secrets. NexusNova can then distinguish a duplicate charge or technical fulfilment problem from an already completed digital service. A refund request is reviewed against the transaction and service state rather than being approved automatically from a browser message alone.

Questions

For refund questions, contact NexusNova Tools at nexusnovatools@gmail.com.
