What is NexusNova Intelligence?

A single interface that routes a natural-language request to the right NexusNova capabilities and presents the result as an understandable workspace.

What should I type?

Describe your goal, not the tool. Example: “I need the cheapest 5-day trip to Istanbul.”

Why show the reasoning?

Recommendations without context feel random. The interface should expose important factors and assumptions.

What happens next?

Every result ends with a clear action: open the tool, refine inputs, compare, save or restart.
