How the Zakat estimate is calculated

The calculator adds the asset values you enter, subtracts the eligible liabilities you enter up to the amount of those assets, and compares the resulting net amount with the selected nisab. If the net amount meets or exceeds that selected threshold, the calculator multiplies it by 2.5%.

Formula: estimated Zakat = (included assets − eligible liabilities) × 2.5%, when the resulting amount meets the selected nisab. This is a mathematical convenience, not an automated religious determination of what you personally owe.

What does Rs 503,529 mean in Pakistan in 2026?

Rs 503,529 is the official Nisab notified for Zakat Year 1446–1447 AH for deduction at source from specified Pakistan saving bank, profit-and-loss sharing and similar accounts. The notification says no such source deduction should be made when the relevant account amount is below Rs 503,529 on the deduction date. NexusNova therefore labels it specifically as a bank-deduction threshold.

That administrative banking threshold should not be confused with a universal answer for every personal Zakat case. A personal calculation may involve cash, precious metals, investments, trade inventory, receivables, debts, ownership duration and other questions.

Why the nisab is editable

The manual option lets you enter the nisab amount you have chosen after applying the religious method appropriate to your circumstances. NexusNova does not automatically choose a gold- or silver-based personal threshold or issue a religious verdict.

What about the lunar year (hawl)?

This first version does not decide whether each asset has completed a required lunar-year holding period. If that question matters to your case, apply the method you follow before entering the amount and seek qualified guidance for complex circumstances.

Sources for the Pakistan bank threshold

  • Pakistan Economic Survey 2025–26 (Government of Pakistan) — records the 1446–47 A.H. Zakat Nisab as Rs 503,529 and notes that the Ministry declares the Nisab through the State Bank of Pakistan and the media.
  • Zakat & Ushr Department (Government of Punjab) FAQ — lists the 1446–47 A.H. (2026) Zakat Nisab as Rs 503,529.

How to use the Pakistan Zakat Calculator

  1. Select or enter the nisab basis and asset values you intend to include, making sure the reference value and date are appropriate for your calculation.
  2. Add eligible cash, gold, savings or other included assets and subtract only liabilities that your chosen method allows.
  3. Review the resulting zakatable amount and cross-check the method with the religious authority or scholar you follow before treating the number as final.

Practical example

If a person has cash savings and gold above the selected nisab threshold, the calculator can total the included assets and apply the configured zakat rate to the eligible amount. The practical answer can change when asset valuation, debt treatment or nisab methodology changes.

Limits and checks

Zakat treatment can differ by scholarly opinion, asset type, ownership period, debt treatment and the gold or silver nisab method. This calculator provides arithmetic assistance, not a religious ruling. Use a trusted scholar or authority for questions about what should be included in your individual case.

Separate mathematical calculation from personal guidance

A Zakat calculator can organize an asset total and apply the selected nisab threshold and rate to the amount used in the calculation. The result is an estimate based on the chosen method; it does not decide the religious or personal treatment of every asset, liability or circumstance.

For practical use, list asset categories and eligible liabilities separately so the calculation can be checked. Cash, savings, gold, trade assets and debts may require different treatment depending on the guidance being followed.

Keep the valuation basis visible

Record the valuation date, nisab reference and method used. For personal circumstances that need interpretation beyond arithmetic, consult a trusted qualified authority rather than relying on a generic calculator alone.
