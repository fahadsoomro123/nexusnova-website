Official Pakistan banking reference

For Zakat Year 1446–1447 AH, the Ministry of Poverty Alleviation and Social Safety notified Rs 503,529 for deduction at source on specified saving, profit-and-loss sharing and similar bank accounts. State Bank of Pakistan circulated the notification in February 2026.

Personal calculation is broader

Whether an asset is zakatable, which liabilities may be deducted, which nisab basis applies, and whether a lunar-year holding condition is satisfied can depend on circumstances and scholarly method. This calculator lets you enter those decisions instead of pretending to make them for you.

Privacy

The amounts entered on this page are calculated in your browser. The calculator does not require an account or send these financial values to a calculation API.

How the Zakat estimate is calculated

The calculator adds the asset values you enter, subtracts the eligible liabilities you enter up to the amount of those assets, and compares the resulting net amount with the selected nisab. If the net amount meets or exceeds that selected threshold, the calculator multiplies it by 2.5%.

Formula: estimated Zakat = (included assets − eligible liabilities) × 2.5%, when the resulting amount meets the selected nisab. This is a mathematical convenience, not an automated religious determination of what you personally owe.

What does Rs 503,529 mean in Pakistan in 2026?

Rs 503,529 is the official Nisab notified for Zakat Year 1446–1447 AH for deduction at source from specified Pakistan saving bank, profit-and-loss sharing and similar accounts. The notification says no such source deduction should be made when the relevant account amount is below Rs 503,529 on the deduction date. NexusNova therefore labels it specifically as a bank-deduction threshold.

That administrative banking threshold should not be confused with a universal answer for every personal Zakat case. A personal calculation may involve cash, precious metals, investments, trade inventory, receivables, debts, ownership duration and other questions.

Why the nisab is editable

The manual option lets you enter the nisab amount you have chosen after applying the religious method appropriate to your circumstances. NexusNova does not automatically choose a gold- or silver-based personal threshold or issue a religious verdict.

What about the lunar year (hawl)?

This first version does not decide whether each asset has completed a required lunar-year holding period. If that question matters to your case, apply the method you follow before entering the amount and seek qualified guidance for complex circumstances.

Sources for the Pakistan bank threshold

  • State Bank of Pakistan BPRD 2026 circular index — lists Circular Letter No. 03 dated 17 February 2026 on Zakat deduction at source.
