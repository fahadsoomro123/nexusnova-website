Comprehensive FAQ Section

Are NexusNova calculator results legally binding?

No. A calculator output is a computational result based on the values and rules implemented by the tool. It does not by itself create a contract, establish a legal right, certify compliance or replace an official determination. For example, a tax estimate, currency conversion, payment calculation or date calculation may help with planning, but a government agency, court, regulator, contract, employer or other authorized authority may use different rules, source records or effective dates. Use the applicable official document or professional advice when the result has legal consequences.

Are files I process with NexusNova Tools stored on your servers?

That depends on the specific tool and its stated processing design. Where a page clearly explains that processing is performed locally in the browser, the intended document transformation occurs in the browser rather than requiring the document to be uploaded to a NexusNova server. However, users should not generalize that statement to every page. Review the tool-specific information and the site privacy policy, and do not upload confidential or sensitive material unless the page clearly explains the relevant handling.

Can I use NexusNova calculators for professional engineering or financial planning?

They may be useful as supporting arithmetic or exploratory tools, but they should not be treated as a substitute for professional engineering analysis, financial planning, accounting work, regulatory review or other specialist processes. An example is a calculator that correctly performs a formula but uses a value that is outdated, rounded differently from an official record or outside the assumptions of a professional model. Critical work should be independently reviewed against the governing standard, source data and professional requirements.

How often are NexusNova tools and information updated?

There is no single update cadence that applies to every page. Some pages are mostly static utilities, while others may depend on changing software behavior, rates, schedules or third-party data. A page may also be updated when an implementation change, source change or content correction is identified. Check the page’s update date, source note, reference date or other context where provided. For time-sensitive decisions, confirm the current value directly with the relevant authoritative source rather than assuming that a page is always current.

Why can two calculators or sources show different results?

Different results can come from different formulas, rounding rules, units, assumptions, exchange rates, timestamps, source datasets or interpretation of the same input. For example, two currency converters can use rates captured at different moments, while two coordinate tools can use different reference conventions. Compare the underlying assumptions and source information instead of treating a small difference as proof that one result is automatically correct.

Can I rely on a NexusNova result for a safety-critical or high-impact decision?

No single browser utility should be treated as the sole authority for a safety-critical or otherwise high-impact decision. Browser, device, data-source and network conditions can affect results, and a tool may not include every real-world factor relevant to the decision. For safety, legal, medical, financial, engineering or regulatory matters, use the appropriate official procedure, professional review or purpose-built equipment in addition to any calculator or reference tool.

What should I do when a result looks wrong?

Recheck the input values, units, spelling, date, source, selected options and assumptions first. Then compare the output with an independent calculation or authoritative reference. If the discrepancy remains, the issue may relate to a source update, implementation limitation, browser condition or an edge case that the tool does not cover. Do not continue relying on a disputed result for an important decision until the discrepancy has been resolved.

Does linking to a third-party service mean NexusNova guarantees that service?

No. External links are provided as references or conveniences and may lead to services whose content, availability, security practices, terms and policies are controlled by the third party. A linked provider can change its API, pricing, documentation or data without notice to NexusNova. Review the third party’s current information directly when its service forms part of your workflow or decision.
