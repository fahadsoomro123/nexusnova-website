Frequently asked questions

Is this a tax return calculator?

No. It illustrates simplified arithmetic and does not classify every transaction or apply every tax rule.

Can exchanging one token for another count as a disposal?

It may. Keep transaction-level records and check official rules for your jurisdiction.

Why might an exchange form differ from this result?

Its basis information may differ or omit other exchanges, wallets or transaction types. Reconcile your records.
