Instant Quote Card Maker

Paste Roman Urdu, Urdu, or English text. Pick a style, generate instantly, then download an HD image for WhatsApp or Instagram.

How to Create HD Status Cards

Turn a short thought, greeting, or original quote into a shareable image without opening a separate design app. This tool offers three visual styles and a large landscape canvas suitable for status updates, posts, and messages. Keep the message focused: a short sentence usually reads more clearly on a phone than a paragraph squeezed into a graphic.

A simple workflow

  • Enter your own quote in the text box. The field accepts up to 220 characters.
  • Choose Dark Gradient, Minimal, or Glassmorphism to match the tone of your message.
  • Select Generate Quote Card to preview the composition. If the text feels crowded, shorten it before downloading.
  • Select Download HD Image to save a PNG, then review it in your gallery before posting.

The exported canvas is 1600 × 900 pixels, a 16:9 layout that stays crisp for most social sharing. The preview is responsive, but the downloaded file uses the full canvas dimensions. Text wrapping depends on character width and available system fonts; unusually long words and mixed scripts may look different across devices.

Frequently asked questions

Which languages are supported?

You can enter English, Urdu, or Roman Urdu. Urdu glyph rendering depends on the fonts available to your browser and device, so inspect the preview before saving.

Is the image high quality?

The PNG export is rendered at 1600 × 900 pixels. It is designed for digital sharing, not as a guarantee of print-specific color or resolution.

Is my quote private?

The page draws the text onto a canvas in your browser and does not send the quote to a NexusNova content-generation server. Avoid including passwords or other sensitive details in shareable artwork, and check the final image before posting.
