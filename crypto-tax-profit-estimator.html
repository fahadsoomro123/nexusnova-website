What this estimate includes and excludes

The simple transaction result is sale proceeds minus adjusted cost basis and disposal fees. A positive result is an illustrative gain; a negative result is shown as a loss and simplified tax for this single disposal is set to zero. Real annual tax depends on all transactions, loss rules, residence, deductions and classification, so one transaction cannot establish a final return.

The US path distinguishes a selected short-term marginal rate from a simplified long-term stacking model using 2026 thresholds. The UK path illustrates the 2026–27 annual exempt amount and common individual CGT rates. Both omit important situations including state and local taxes, NIIT, staking or mining income, special reliefs and complex matching rules.

Maintain exchange exports, wallet transfers, acquisition basis, timestamps, fees and currency conversions. Use official IRS and HMRC sources in the crypto recordkeeping guide, then verify the full-year result with qualified advice. This tool is informational, not tax advice.

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculate my complete annual tax bill?

No. It models a single disposal with simplified assumptions. Other transactions, losses, income, state taxes and special rules can materially change the liability.

Why does the holding period matter in the US?

Long-term federal gains can use preferential thresholds. Short-term gains generally follow ordinary income rates. Verify your actual holding dates and tax status.

Are crypto staking rewards included?

No. Mining, staking, rewards and business activity can have separate income-tax treatment and need separate records and advice.
