Tool methodology

A practical workflow for a more controllable prompt

Start by writing the outcome in one sentence, then separate the subject, action, setting, framing, lighting and constraints. A useful brief tells a creator what the viewer should notice first. “A ceramic coffee cup on a rain-darkened windowsill, soft morning side light, close framing and empty space to the left for a headline” is easier to evaluate than a stack of mood words. Use only details that help the composition or intended use; conflicting directions make iteration less informative.

Generate a baseline before adding elaborate style references. Inspect whether the focal subject is visible, whether the composition suits the final crop and whether the result tells the intended story. If the composition is wrong, change framing first. If the atmosphere is wrong, adjust lighting or palette. Changing every field at once makes it harder to learn which instruction mattered. Save the exact prompt and a note about the result so you can repeat a useful approach later.

Example: turn a vague concept into a shot brief

For a Midjourney still image, an idea such as “futuristic city” can be narrowed to “a pedestrian-scale street in a coastal future city at blue hour, reflective stone, low glass transit shelters, one clear human focal point, wide editorial composition and restrained cyan lighting.” Add an aspect ratio that matches the destination. For a Sora video, describe time instead: the person enters the frame, pauses beside a shelter, looks toward the sea, and the camera slowly tracks sideways. State which elements stay consistent. A temporal instruction gives you something specific to inspect, even though model output is never guaranteed.

Review rights, text and continuity before publishing

Keep exact legal labels, prices and brand copy out of generated imagery when accuracy matters; add and proofread those details in an editor. Check reference-material rights and consent for recognizable people, and avoid presenting generated footage as authentic evidence. Do not place confidential client information into prompts unless its use is authorized and the service’s data handling is appropriate. Review the current official Midjourney prompt guidance and OpenAI Help Center because controls and model behavior can change. The enhancer formats your text locally; it does not send prompts to either provider or guarantee a particular output.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool send my idea to Midjourney or Sora?

No. It assembles text in your browser and does not submit content to a generation service.

Will the prompt guarantee a particular result?

No. Results depend on the platform and model. Use the prompt as a structured brief and iterate.

Should I put critical text inside generated video?

For important labels or product copy, add and verify text during editing rather than relying on generated frames.
