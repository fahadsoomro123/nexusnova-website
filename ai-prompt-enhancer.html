Build prompts you can evaluate

Begin with one observable subject and a specific visual outcome. Add framing, light, palette and only constraints that will change the result. For video, describe one main action and one camera move with a clear start and end. A long list of mood adjectives often adds less value than a concrete composition or motion cue.

Generate a first version, inspect what is missing, and revise one dimension at a time. If framing fails, change framing before rewriting the subject. If motion becomes chaotic, simplify the action. Keep the strong version and note the change so the next generation is a test rather than a guess.

This tool formats your brief locally and does not send prompts to an AI model or guarantee output quality. Review target-model documentation and rights for reference materials before publication. Read the AI prompt workflow guide for composition and continuity advice.

Worked example: from vague to actionable

A vague brief such as “a futuristic city, cinematic” leaves the subject, camera position, lighting and focal point unresolved. A more testable image brief might specify “a rainy elevated street at blue hour, one compact electric taxi in the foreground, reflective asphalt, a warm shop window behind it, wide establishing composition, camera low to the pavement, cyan shadows and amber practical lights, empty right third for later headline placement.” The second description states what should be visible and where the viewer should look.

For a video version, replace the frozen composition with a short action: “the taxi rolls slowly through a shallow puddle, water ripples outward, the camera tracks beside the vehicle and comes to rest on the lit passenger window.” Keep the same subject, environment, palette and screen direction between related shots. If the result is cluttered, remove secondary events before adding more adjectives. If the object is correct but the light is wrong, edit only the lighting instruction and compare again.

Before you export

Check whether the prompt names one dominant subject, one visual hierarchy, an appropriate aspect ratio and constraints you can actually verify. For video, check whether the movement fits the duration and avoids contradictory camera instructions. For commercial use, review the generated output, reference rights, product accuracy and the relevant model’s current terms. This enhancer formats text locally; it does not create an image or video, query an AI model, validate licensing or predict how an audience will react.

Frequently asked questions

Does this tool call Midjourney or Sora?

No. It formats prompt text in your browser and does not call an AI service or consume model credits.

What makes a prompt easier to follow?

Name the subject, composition, lighting and key constraints. For video, specify one main action and a clear camera move.

Does a better prompt guarantee a good image or video?

No. Model version, randomness, references and post-production affect the result. Review outputs against your requirements.
