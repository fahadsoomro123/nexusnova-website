Has the official 2027 Social Security COLA been announced?

As of the October 14, 2026 source check, the SSA's public COLA summary still displayed the 2.8% COLA determined on October 24, 2025 and said the next COLA would be announced in October 2026. It did not list a final 2027 rate on that page. Do not treat any percentage in the calculator below as official unless it appears in a later SSA announcement.

The difference between the official annual adjustment and the SSA Trustees projection matters. The Trustees Report uses assumptions to model Social Security’s finances over many years. The annual COLA, by contrast, is determined from observed third-quarter Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W) data. The 2.4% Trustees figure is a long-range assumption, not a competing official 2027 rate.

How much could different COLA scenarios add?

Use your own current monthly benefit to compare hypothetical percentages. For a $2,000 benefit, 3.6% would add $72 per month, 3.5% would add $70, and 3.4% would add $68 before any other changes or deductions. These figures are simple arithmetic examples, not a forecast or the SSA's official 2027 COLA.

How the SSA calculates COLA

The annual adjustment is based on the change in the average CPI-W for July, August and September compared with the average for the third quarter of the last year in which a COLA became effective. The percentage change is rounded to the nearest tenth of a percentage point. This is why the September inflation release is a key date: the full third-quarter data set is needed to determine the adjustment.

The Bureau of Labor Statistics released September 2026 CPI data on October 14. The CPI release supplies data used in inflation analysis; it is not itself the SSA's published COLA determination. On the source check date, the SSA's COLA summary still said the next rate would be announced in October 2026.

When would a 2027 COLA take effect?

Social Security’s annual COLA is effective for December of the current year and is generally reflected in benefits payable in January of the next year. The usual schedule makes a COLA effective for December and payable in January of the following year, but the exact 2027 percentage and official notice should be confirmed directly with SSA once published. Supplemental Security Income timing can differ, so recipients should check their own SSA notices.

What to check when SSA publishes the rate

After SSA publishes the rate, compare the official percentage with any estimates you have seen. Then check how Medicare premiums, tax withholding or other benefit deductions could affect the amount actually deposited. A COLA does not guarantee that a person's net bank deposit rises by the same dollar amount.
