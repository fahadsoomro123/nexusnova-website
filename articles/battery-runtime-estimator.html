Battery Runtime: Estimate Hours from Capacity and Load

Estimate runtime from usable energy and average load while accounting for efficiency, temperature, age and reserve.

Estimate runtime from usable energy and average load while accounting for efficiency, temperature, age and reserve. Usable capacity, efficiency and discharge limits vary; follow manufacturer safety instructions.

A useful estimate makes its assumptions visible. Record the date, units, source and period for each input. Replace illustrative values with your own current statement, quote, contract, meter or budget before using the result to make a commitment.

Formula and inputs

Formula: Runtime ≈ usable Wh × conversion efficiency ÷ load watts

Keep the inputs on the same basis. Mixing monthly and annual periods, unlike units, gross and take-home income, or fixed and per-unit charges can make correct arithmetic misleading. When an input is uncertain, model a range rather than hiding it.

Worked example

A 500 Wh battery through a 90%-efficient inverter powering 100 W gives about 4.5 hours before degradation and reserve.

Important limitation: Usable capacity, efficiency and discharge limits vary; follow manufacturer safety instructions.

How to apply the result

List loads and hours, convert capacity to watt-hours, apply usable fraction and efficiency and follow the manufacturer's safety rules.

  1. Write down current inputs and their dates.
  2. Verify the rate, fee, unit, eligibility and timing with a primary source.
  3. Test a conservative case and a plausible alternative while changing one variable at a time.
  4. Compare the estimate with an actual bill, statement, meter reading, quote or contract.

Common mistakes

  • Treating nominal capacity as delivered energy: Allow for usable fraction and losses.
  • Ignoring peak demand: Check continuous and surge limits.
  • Copying an example unchanged: illustrative figures explain the method; they do not replace your own data.
  • Comparing different terms: dates, units and included charges must match.
