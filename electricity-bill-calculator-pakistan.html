What the quick estimate includes

The result combines the notified base energy charge with an estimated fixed charge. In quick mode, 3 kW is only a disclosed planning assumption; change it under Advanced bill details when your bill shows another sanctioned load.

Why category matters

Lifeline, Protected and Unprotected consumers use different tariff treatment. Protected eligibility also depends on historical consumption rules, so NexusNova does not infer that status from a single month's units. When you select “I don't know”, the quick estimate uses Unprotected and clearly labels that assumption.

Why your printed bill can differ

FCA/FPA, quarterly tariff adjustments, GST, electricity duty, PTV fee, arrears, credits, meter-specific items and later official revisions can change the payable amount. Advanced fields let you enter the signed values printed on your own bill rather than relying on a guessed current adjustment.
