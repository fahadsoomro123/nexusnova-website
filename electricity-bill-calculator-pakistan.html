Why the result may differ from your bill

Fuel Charge Adjustment, quarterly tariff adjustments, taxes, electricity duty, PTV fee, arrears, credits, meter-specific items and temporary relief can change the payable amount. This tool does not invent those values.

Protected vs unprotected matters

The notified tariff gives protected residential consumers the benefit of one previous slab. Unprotected consumers do not receive that benefit, so the rate of the monthly landing slab applies to all units.

Private calculation

Your unit, load and bill values are calculated in the browser. No electricity account number or bill data is uploaded to a calculation API.

How slab math is handled

Lifeline: the selected lifeline band is applied without a previous-slab benefit. Protected: when usage is 101–200 units, the calculator preserves the one-previous-slab benefit by charging the first 100 units at the protected 1–100 rate and the remainder at the protected 101–200 rate. Unprotected: the rate of the band reached by the month's total is applied to all monthly units.

How fixed charges are estimated

For Non-ToU residential consumers, the notified decision states that fixed charges are based on sanctioned load. For Time-of-Use residential consumers, the applicable load is the higher of 50% of sanctioned load or the month's MDI, then multiplied by Rs 675/kW/month.

Why FCA and QTA are manual

Fuel Charge Adjustment can change month to month and quarterly tariff adjustments apply for defined billing windows. A figure that was correct for an earlier bill can be wrong for a later bill. The calculator therefore defaults these fields to zero and lets you enter the exact signed rates from the bill you are estimating.
