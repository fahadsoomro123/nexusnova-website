AVIF to JPG Converter

Convert an AVIF image into a widely supported JPG file locally in a modern browser, with a simple quality control for the JPEG output.

Why convert AVIF to JPG?

AVIF is compact and modern, but some older apps, upload forms and editing workflows still work more reliably with JPEG.

Browser requirement

The browser must be able to decode the selected AVIF image. Current modern browsers generally support AVIF; an older browser may need an update.

Transparent AVIF files

JPEG does not support transparency. Transparent pixels are placed on a white background in the JPG output.

Choosing JPG output from an AVIF image

AVIF is designed for efficient modern image compression, while JPG remains widely compatible with older apps, websites and document workflows. Convert to JPG when compatibility matters more than preserving AVIF features such as very efficient compression or transparency.

JPG uses lossy compression, so repeated conversions can reduce detail. Keep the original AVIF when possible and convert a copy for sharing. Photographs usually tolerate JPG well, while logos, screenshots and graphics with sharp text may show artifacts at aggressive compression levels.

Practical checks

  • Keep an untouched original before converting.
  • Check fine text, edges and gradients after export.
  • Use PNG instead when you must preserve transparency.

Quick FAQ

Will AVIF to JPG keep transparency? No. Standard JPG does not support an alpha transparency channel. Transparent areas must be flattened against a background, so review the converted image before publishing or sending it.
