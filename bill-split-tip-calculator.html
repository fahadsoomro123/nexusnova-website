Bill Split & Tip Calculator

Calculate a tip, total bill and equal amount per person in seconds.

Enter a bill amount.

How it works

The tip is calculated as a percentage of the bill. The final total is the bill plus the tip, then divided equally by the number of people.

Worked example

If a restaurant bill is 5,000 and you choose a 10% tip, the tip is 500 and the total becomes 5,500. Split equally between four people, each person pays 1,375. The calculator keeps the same arithmetic visible so you can compare another tip percentage or group size without doing the steps manually.

Useful for restaurants and shared expenses

Use the calculator when a group wants an equal split, when you need to compare different tip percentages, or when you want to check each person’s share before paying. It can also help with shared delivery or outing expenses when every participant is paying the same proportion.

What this calculator does not handle

The result assumes an equal split. It does not assign different menu items to different people, calculate tax rules, service charges or currency conversion automatically. If the bill already includes a service charge, confirm whether you still want to add a separate tip before entering a percentage.

Rounding can also matter when a total cannot be divided perfectly into equal currency units. Use the displayed result as the mathematical split, then adjust the final small rounding difference among the group if necessary.

Bill Split & Tip Calculator: clear guide for real use

The Bill Split & Tip Calculator divides a bill across a chosen number of people and can add a tip percentage to the shared total. It is useful for quickly comparing the base share, tip amount and per-person payment without doing several calculations manually.

How to use Bill Split & Tip Calculator

  1. Read the labels and confirm every number is in the unit or format expected by Bill Split & Tip Calculator before calculating.
  2. Enter the source values in Bill Split & Tip Calculator, run the calculation, and keep the original inputs visible while you review the result.
  3. Check the result from Bill Split & Tip Calculator against the real question you are answering, including the correct base value, period, unit and rounding rule.

Practical example

For a 10,000 bill split between four people with a 10% tip, the tip is 1,000 and the total becomes 11,000. An equal split gives 2,750 per person, assuming everyone shares the bill and tip equally.

Limits, privacy and checks

Check whether tax, service charge or delivery fees are already included before adding another percentage. Real groups may not split equally when people ordered different items, so use the calculator only for the sharing rule you actually agreed on.
