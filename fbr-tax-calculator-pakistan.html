What this calculator answers

This tool answers a simple planning question: based on annual taxable income and income category, what tax does the selected slab table produce? It is deliberately simpler than a full return because real tax liability can depend on income heads, deductions, credits, withholding, minimum or final tax, special regimes and other legal details.

Salary and non-salaried income use different tables

FBR distinguishes salaried individuals from other individual and AOP income categories. For Tax Year 2027, the salaried table uses the Finance Act 2026 structure, while the non-salaried table has its own progressive bands. Selecting the wrong category can change the result.

Salary example

An annual taxable salary of Rs 1.2 million sits at the upper end of the second salaried band. The progressive formula charges the marginal rate only on the amount above the lower threshold rather than applying that rate to the entire salary.

Business example

For a sole proprietor or other non-salaried individual, business taxable income is not automatically the same as total sales. The relevant taxable amount can depend on allowable expenses and other statutory adjustments. This quick tool does not decide those inputs.

Do not treat the result as a tax return. The calculator does not file with FBR, generate a PSID, calculate every withholding or special regime, or determine the legal treatment of your personal circumstances.
