What is the official 2027 Social Security COLA?

A cost-of-living adjustment is intended to help Social Security and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits keep pace with measured changes in consumer prices. The SSA determines the annual COLA using the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W). The annual adjustment is set under the rules described by the agency, not by a calculator or a media forecast.

The Social Security Administration announced a 3.6% COLA for 2027 on October 14, 2026. The agency says nearly 72 million Social Security beneficiaries will see the increase in benefits payable in January 2027, while increased payments to about 7.3 million SSI recipients begin December 31, 2026. Read the SSA announcement and official 2027 fact sheet for the published details.
