What is the 2027 Social Security COLA?

A cost-of-living adjustment is intended to help Social Security and Supplemental Security Income (SSI) benefits keep pace with measured changes in consumer prices. The SSA determines the annual COLA using the Consumer Price Index for Urban Wage Earners and Clerical Workers (CPI-W). The annual adjustment is set under the rules described by the agency, not by a calculator or a media forecast.

At the time this page was checked, the SSA's public COLA summary says the next adjustment will be announced in October 2026. The 2026 Trustees Report lists a 2.4% estimate for 2027, while the actual 2026 COLA was 2.8%. An actuarial assumption is not the official announcement, so the calculator labels each option and allows you to enter a different rate.

Why your deposited payment may not rise by the same amount

This calculator estimates the gross benefit change; it cannot calculate your personal net payment. The amount you receive can differ because of Medicare premiums, tax withholding, benefit-specific rules, overpayment recovery or other deductions. SSI and Social Security retirement, survivor and disability benefits also have different details. For a personal amount, use the SSA's official notice or your my Social Security account.

Frequently asked questions

Has the 2027 Social Security COLA been officially announced?

As of October 12, 2026, the SSA's public COLA summary said the next COLA would be announced in October 2026. This page therefore labels the 2.4% 2027 figure as a projection rather than a final rate. Check the SSA source links below for the latest official status.

Where does the 2.4% figure come from?

It appears in the SSA Office of the Chief Actuary's 2026 Trustees Report assumptions table as an estimated 2027 COLA. The agency can announce a different actual adjustment after applying the statutory formula to the relevant price-index data.

Is the 2.8% option the 2027 rate?

No. The SSA announced a 2.8% COLA for 2026 on October 24, 2025. That option is included only as a historical comparison to show how different percentages affect the same benefit amount.

Does the calculator check my Social Security account?

No. It runs the calculation in your browser using the amount and rate you enter. It does not ask for a Social Security number, sign in to an SSA account, retrieve records or store your input on a server.
